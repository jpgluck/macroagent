**BUSINESS MANAGEMENT AND REVENUE-SHARING AGREEMENT**

This Business Management and Revenue-Sharing Agreement (the "Agreement") is entered into as of **[DATE]** (the "Effective Date") by and between:

**Coach:** [COACH FULL LEGAL NAME], an individual residing at [COACH ADDRESS], [CITY, STATE, ZIP] ("Coach"); and

**Manager:** [MANAGER FULL LEGAL NAME / BUSINESS ENTITY NAME], [an individual residing at / a [STATE] [entity type] with its principal place of business at] [MANAGER ADDRESS], [CITY, STATE, ZIP] ("Manager").

Coach and Manager are referred to individually as a "Party" and collectively as the "Parties."

## RECITALS

WHEREAS, Coach operates a running coaching business under the brand name **[BRAND NAME]** and desires to grow revenue streams including but not limited to a newsletter, podcast, digital products, affiliate programs, and brand partnerships;

WHEREAS, Manager has expertise in business development, revenue operations, content monetization, and partnership management and desires to provide such services to Coach in exchange for a revenue-sharing arrangement;

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants and promises set forth herein, and for other good and valuable consideration, the receipt and sufficiency of which are hereby acknowledged, the Parties agree as follows:

---

## ARTICLE 1 — SERVICES

**1.1 Scope of Services.** Manager agrees to provide business management and development services to Coach, which may include, without limitation: newsletter monetization and growth strategy, affiliate program management, digital product development and sales, podcast sponsorship outreach and account management, brand deal and partnership negotiation and execution, revenue reporting and dashboard management, and related operational support (collectively, the "Services").

**1.2 Performance Standard.** Manager agrees to perform the Services professionally, in good faith, and in a manner consistent with industry standards for business management services.

**1.3 Coach's Cooperation.** Coach agrees to provide Manager with reasonable access to information, platforms, and resources necessary for Manager to perform the Services, including but not limited to analytics data, content calendars, and audience metrics.

**1.4 Independent Contractor.** Manager is an independent contractor and not an employee, partner, agent, or joint venturer of Coach. Manager has no authority to bind Coach to any contract or obligation without Coach's prior written approval. Manager is solely responsible for all taxes, insurance, and other obligations arising from Manager's compensation under this Agreement.

---

## ARTICLE 2 — TERM AND RENEWAL

**2.1 Initial Term.** This Agreement commences on the Effective Date and continues for a period of twelve (12) months (the "Initial Term"), unless earlier terminated in accordance with Article 5.

**2.2 Automatic Renewal.** Upon expiration of the Initial Term, this Agreement shall automatically renew for successive one-year periods (each a "Renewal Term," and together with the Initial Term, the "Term") unless either Party provides the other with written notice of non-renewal at least ninety (90) days prior to the end of the then-current term.

**2.3 Ramp-Up Protection.** During the first six (6) months of the Initial Term (the "Ramp-Up Period"), Coach may not terminate this Agreement without Cause (as defined in Section 5.2). This protection exists to allow Manager a reasonable period in which to invest time, relationships, and effort in building the revenue streams contemplated by this Agreement. Nothing in this Section limits Coach's right to terminate for Cause during the Ramp-Up Period.

---

## ARTICLE 3 — REVENUE SHARING

**3.1 Revenue Streams and Splits.** In lieu of a fixed fee, Coach agrees to pay Manager a percentage of gross revenue (prior to expenses, except as set forth in Section 3.3) generated from the following revenue streams:

| Revenue Stream | Manager's Share | Coach's Share |
|---|---|---|
| Newsletter (advertising, sponsorships, paid subscriptions) | 50% | 50% |
| Affiliate marketing (all platforms and programs) | 50% | 50% |
| Digital products (courses, guides, training plans, templates) | 50% | 50% |
| Podcast sponsorships and in-episode advertising | 30% | 70% |
| Brand deals and partnerships (endorsements, sponsored content, ambassadorships) | 25% | 75% |
| Coaching revenue **growth above Baseline** (as defined in Section 3.2) | 10% | 90% |

**3.2 Coaching Revenue Baseline; Excluded Coaching Revenue.**

(a) **Baseline Definition.** "Baseline Coaching Revenue" means the total gross monthly coaching revenue earned by Coach as of the Effective Date, as documented in Exhibit A attached hereto and incorporated by reference. The Parties shall each sign Exhibit A concurrently with this Agreement.

(b) **Manager's Share — Coaching Growth Only.** Manager's 10% share applies only to monthly coaching revenue that exceeds the Baseline Coaching Revenue in any given calendar month. For example, if the Baseline is $[X] per month and Coach earns $[X + $2,000] in a given month, Manager's 10% applies only to the $2,000 in incremental revenue.

(c) **Excluded Coaching Clients.** All existing 1:1 coaching clients of Coach as of the Effective Date, and their existing contracted rates, are expressly excluded from any revenue sharing arrangement. Revenue from these clients is 100% retained by Coach regardless of any coaching revenue growth calculation. The list of excluded clients and their current rates shall be maintained by Coach. Coach agrees to notify Manager in writing if any excluded client upgrades to a new program or rate structure, in which case the new incremental revenue above the excluded rate may be included in the growth calculation.

**3.3 Expense Deductions.**

(a) **Pre-Split Deductions.** Reasonable and necessary business expenses directly attributable to the revenue streams managed by Manager ("Business Expenses") shall be deducted from gross revenue before the revenue split is calculated. Business Expenses include, without limitation, software subscriptions (e.g., Beehiiv, Canva, ConvertKit, design tools), transaction processing fees, and other direct costs of revenue generation.

(b) **Approval Threshold.** Business Expenses up to $100 per month in aggregate may be incurred by Manager without prior approval. Any single expense or category of expenses that will cause total monthly Business Expenses to exceed $100 requires the prior mutual written approval of both Parties (email confirmation is sufficient).

(c) **Documentation.** Manager shall maintain reasonable records of all Business Expenses and include them in the monthly revenue report described in Article 4.

**3.4 No Guaranteed Revenue.** The Parties acknowledge that revenue under this Agreement is variable and contingent upon market conditions, audience growth, and other factors outside either Party's control. Nothing in this Agreement guarantees any minimum revenue to either Party.

---

## ARTICLE 4 — REPORTING AND PAYMENT

**4.1 Monthly Revenue Report.** Within ten (10) days following the end of each calendar month, Manager shall prepare and deliver to Coach a written revenue report (the "Monthly Report") setting forth: (a) gross revenue by stream for the prior month; (b) itemized Business Expenses; (c) net revenue by stream after expense deductions; (d) the calculated revenue share owed to Manager; and (e) cumulative year-to-date figures.

**4.2 Shared Dashboard.** The Parties agree to maintain a shared reporting dashboard or spreadsheet (accessible to both Parties) that tracks revenue, expenses, and payments in real time to the extent practicable. Manager is responsible for keeping this dashboard current.

**4.3 Payment Due Date.** Coach shall pay Manager the revenue share amounts set forth in the Monthly Report within fifteen (15) days of the end of each calendar month (i.e., within five (5) days of receiving the Monthly Report). Payment shall be made via **[PAYMENT METHOD: e.g., ACH transfer, PayPal, Venmo Business, check]** to the account or address designated by Manager in writing.

**4.4 Disputed Amounts.** If Coach disputes any line item in a Monthly Report, Coach shall notify Manager in writing within five (5) business days of receipt, identifying the specific item(s) in dispute and the basis for the dispute. The Parties shall work in good faith to resolve disputes within ten (10) days. Undisputed amounts shall be paid by the due date regardless of any pending dispute.

**4.5 Audit Rights.** Each Party shall have the right, upon fifteen (15) days' prior written notice, to audit the other Party's books and records relevant to revenue calculations under this Agreement, no more than once per calendar year. Audits shall be conducted during normal business hours and at the requesting Party's expense, unless the audit reveals an underpayment of more than 5%, in which case the audited Party shall bear the reasonable cost of the audit.

**4.6 Late Payments.** Payments not made by the due date shall accrue interest at a rate of **[X]%** per month (or the maximum rate permitted by applicable law, whichever is less) from the due date until paid in full.

## ARTICLE 5 — TERMINATION

**5.1 Termination Without Cause.** After the Ramp-Up Period (Section 2.3), either Party may terminate this Agreement without cause by providing the other Party with sixty (60) days' prior written notice. During the notice period, both Parties shall continue to fulfill their obligations under this Agreement.

**5.2 Termination for Cause.** Either Party may terminate this Agreement immediately upon written notice if the other Party: (a) materially breaches this Agreement and fails to cure such breach within fifteen (15) business days of receiving written notice describing the breach in reasonable detail; (b) becomes insolvent, makes an assignment for the benefit of creditors, or becomes subject to bankruptcy or receivership proceedings; or (c) engages in fraud, gross negligence, or willful misconduct in connection with this Agreement ("Cause").

**5.3 Trailing Revenue — Manager's Post-Termination Share.**

(a) **Trailing Period.** Regardless of the reason for termination (other than termination of Manager for Cause under Section 5.2), Manager shall continue to receive their full revenue share percentages as set forth in Section 3.1 for a period of six (6) months following the effective termination date (the "Trailing Period").

(b) **Scope.** The Trailing Period revenue share applies to all revenue streams that Manager built, managed, or materially contributed to during the Term. This includes, without limitation, newsletter subscribers acquired during the Term, affiliate relationships established during the Term, digital products created during the Term, and podcast or brand sponsorships executed or negotiated during the Term.

(c) **Termination for Cause — Manager.** If Manager is terminated for Cause, the Trailing Period does not apply, and Manager's right to revenue share ceases as of the termination date, except for amounts already earned and owed through the termination date.

(d) **Termination for Cause — Coach.** If Coach is terminated or Manager terminates for cause attributable to Coach, the Trailing Period applies in full.

(e) **Reporting During Trailing Period.** Coach shall continue to provide Monthly Reports to Manager (or provide Manager with access to sufficient information to calculate the revenue share) during the Trailing Period, and payments shall remain subject to the terms of Article 4.

**5.4 Effect of Termination.** Upon termination, and following the conclusion of any applicable Trailing Period: (a) Manager shall return or destroy Coach's confidential information and provide access credentials to all accounts managed on Coach's behalf; (b) Coach shall pay all undisputed amounts owed through the termination date and through the Trailing Period; and (c) all license rights granted hereunder shall terminate except as needed for the orderly wind-down.

## ARTICLE 6 — INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY AND ASSET OWNERSHIP

**6.1 Coach's Content.** All content created solely by Coach, including written articles, videos, audio recordings, social media posts, training materials derived solely from Coach's expertise and methodology, and other original works of authorship authored by Coach, are and shall remain the exclusive property of Coach.

**6.2 Email Subscriber List.** The email subscriber list(s) associated with Coach's newsletter and any other owned media are the exclusive property of Coach's brand and business. Manager acknowledges that it has no ownership interest in the subscriber list, and upon termination, Manager shall not retain, use, or transfer any subscriber data.

**6.3 Jointly Created Digital Products.** Digital products, systems, templates, frameworks, and other works that are created collaboratively by both Parties during the Term (e.g., training plan templates, course curricula, lead magnets) shall be jointly owned by both Parties (50/50), with each Party having the right to use, reproduce, and distribute such works without accounting to the other, except as otherwise agreed in a separate written instrument. Notwithstanding the foregoing, Coach retains the exclusive right to use jointly owned works under Coach's brand name.

**6.4 Manager-Developed Systems.** Proprietary operational systems, workflows, templates, or tools developed by Manager independently (i.e., not using Coach's confidential information) that are used in connection with the Services but are not tailored exclusively to Coach shall remain Manager's property. Manager grants Coach a non-exclusive, royalty-free license to continue using such systems during the Trailing Period and thereafter only if the Parties agree in writing.

**6.5 Sponsor and Brand Relationships.**

(a) Manager acknowledges that relationships with sponsors and brand partners developed through Coach's platform, audience, and media properties are business assets of Coach's brand.

(b) However, personal relationships and goodwill developed by Manager with specific sponsor contacts during the Term are acknowledged to be professional assets of Manager.

(c) For a period of twelve (12) months following the termination of this Agreement, Coach shall not directly contact any sponsor or brand partner contact that was sourced, introduced, or primarily managed by Manager for purposes of renegotiating, renewing, or entering into new agreements with such contacts, without Manager's prior written consent. This restriction does not apply to contacts that Coach had a pre-existing relationship with prior to the Effective Date (as documented in writing at or before the Effective Date).

**6.6 No License Beyond Scope.** Except as expressly set forth in this Agreement, neither Party grants the other any license or right in its intellectual property.

## ARTICLE 7 — RIGHT OF FIRST REFUSAL

**7.1 ROFR Trigger.** If Coach desires to engage any third party to perform services substantially similar to the Services described in Article 1 during the Term (whether as a replacement for Manager or as a parallel engagement), Coach shall first provide Manager with written notice of Coach's intent, including a description of the proposed services and the material terms (including compensation) proposed to such third party (the "ROFR Notice").

**7.2 Exercise Period.** Manager shall have thirty (30) days following receipt of the ROFR Notice (the "ROFR Period") to notify Coach in writing whether Manager elects to perform the substantially similar services on the same material terms as proposed to the third party.

**7.3 Match or Decline.** If Manager timely elects to match, the Parties shall negotiate in good faith to amend this Agreement to reflect the new terms within fifteen (15) days of Manager's election. If Manager declines or fails to respond within the ROFR Period, Coach may engage the third party on terms no more favorable to the third party than those disclosed in the ROFR Notice for a period of ninety (90) days thereafter.

**7.4 Limitations.** This right of first refusal does not apply to: (a) additional employees or contractors hired by Coach to assist with content creation, social media, or other services clearly outside the scope of the Services; or (b) platform-specific tools or agencies engaged for narrow, specialized functions (e.g., podcast editing, graphic design).

---

## ARTICLE 8 — NON-COMPETE AND NON-SOLICITATION

**8.1 Manager's Non-Compete.** During the Term, Manager agrees not to provide business management or revenue development services (substantially similar to the Services) to any running coach who: (a) competes directly with Coach; (b) targets substantially the same geographic market as Coach; and (c) offers substantially similar coaching services to substantially similar clientele. For clarity, Manager may work with clients in non-competing niches (e.g., other sports, general fitness, nutrition coaching) and may work with running coaches who operate in clearly distinct geographic markets or serve materially different demographics.

**8.2 Coach's Non-Solicitation.** During the Term and for twelve (12) months following termination, Coach agrees not to directly solicit or encourage any of Manager's other clients, vendors, or contractors to terminate or reduce their relationship with Manager.

**8.3 Manager's Non-Solicitation.** During the Term and for twelve (12) months following termination, Manager agrees not to directly solicit Coach's existing coaching clients or newsletter subscribers for Manager's own competing business ventures.

**8.4 Reasonableness.** The Parties acknowledge that the scope, duration, and geographic limitations of the covenants in this Article are reasonable and necessary to protect the legitimate business interests of each Party. If any court of competent jurisdiction finds any provision of this Article to be unenforceable, the court is authorized to modify such provision to the minimum extent necessary to make it enforceable.

## ARTICLE 9 — CONFIDENTIALITY

**9.1 Definition.** "Confidential Information" means any non-public information disclosed by one Party (the "Disclosing Party") to the other (the "Receiving Party") in connection with this Agreement that is designated as confidential or that reasonably should be understood to be confidential given the nature of the information and the circumstances of disclosure. Confidential Information includes, without limitation: business financials, revenue data, client lists and client information, pricing strategies, sponsor rates and deal terms, marketing and content strategies, subscriber and audience data, proprietary methodologies, and the terms of this Agreement.

**9.2 Obligations.** Each Receiving Party agrees to: (a) hold the Disclosing Party's Confidential Information in strict confidence; (b) not disclose Confidential Information to any third party without the Disclosing Party's prior written consent; and (c) use Confidential Information solely for purposes of performing obligations under this Agreement.

**9.3 Exceptions.** Confidentiality obligations do not apply to information that: (a) is or becomes publicly available through no fault of the Receiving Party; (b) was rightfully known by the Receiving Party prior to disclosure; (c) is independently developed by the Receiving Party without use of the Disclosing Party's Confidential Information; or (d) is required to be disclosed by law or valid legal process, provided that the Receiving Party gives prompt written notice to the Disclosing Party to allow the Disclosing Party to seek a protective order.

**9.4 Survival.** Confidentiality obligations shall survive termination of this Agreement for a period of three (3) years following termination.

## ARTICLE 10 — DISPUTE RESOLUTION

**10.1 Good Faith Negotiation.** In the event of any dispute arising out of or relating to this Agreement, the Parties shall first attempt to resolve the dispute through good faith negotiation for a period of fifteen (15) days following written notice of the dispute from one Party to the other.

**10.2 Mediation.** If the dispute is not resolved through negotiation, the Parties agree to submit the dispute to non-binding mediation administered by a mutually agreed mediator before filing for arbitration. The costs of mediation shall be shared equally by the Parties.

**10.3 Binding Arbitration.** If mediation does not resolve the dispute within thirty (30) days of the commencement of mediation (or such longer period as the Parties may agree), the dispute shall be finally resolved by binding arbitration administered by **[ARBITRATION ORGANIZATION, e.g., JAMS or AAA]** in accordance with its then-current commercial arbitration rules. The arbitration shall be conducted by a single arbitrator agreed upon by the Parties or, failing agreement, appointed by the administering organization. The seat of arbitration shall be **[CITY, STATE]**. The arbitrator's award shall be final and binding and may be entered as a judgment in any court of competent jurisdiction.

**10.4 Injunctive Relief.** Notwithstanding the foregoing, either Party may seek emergency injunctive or other equitable relief from a court of competent jurisdiction to prevent irreparable harm pending resolution of a dispute, without waiving the right to arbitration.

**10.5 Costs.** The prevailing Party in any arbitration proceeding shall be entitled to recover its reasonable attorneys' fees and arbitration costs from the non-prevailing Party, as determined by the arbitrator.

## ARTICLE 11 — GENERAL PROVISIONS

**11.1 Governing Law.** This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the State of **[STATE]**, without regard to its conflict of laws principles.

**11.2 Entire Agreement.** This Agreement, including all Exhibits, constitutes the entire agreement between the Parties with respect to its subject matter and supersedes all prior negotiations, representations, warranties, and understandings of the Parties, whether oral or written, with respect to such subject matter.

**11.3 Amendments.** This Agreement may not be amended or modified except by a written instrument signed by both Parties.

**11.4 Waiver.** The failure of either Party to enforce any right or provision of this Agreement shall not constitute a waiver of such right or provision. Any waiver must be in writing and signed by the waiving Party.

**11.5 Severability.** If any provision of this Agreement is found by a court or arbitrator of competent jurisdiction to be unenforceable, that provision shall be modified to the minimum extent necessary to make it enforceable, and the remaining provisions shall continue in full force and effect.

**11.6 Notices.** All notices required or permitted under this Agreement shall be in writing and delivered by: (a) hand delivery; (b) nationally recognized overnight courier; (c) certified mail, return receipt requested; or (d) email with confirmation of receipt, to the addresses set forth in the preamble or such other address as a Party may designate in writing. Notice is effective upon actual receipt.

**11.7 No Assignment.** Neither Party may assign this Agreement or any rights or obligations hereunder without the prior written consent of the other Party, which shall not be unreasonably withheld. Any purported assignment in violation of this Section is void.

**11.8 Force Majeure.** Neither Party shall be in default for failure to perform obligations if such failure is caused by circumstances beyond the Party's reasonable control, including acts of God, natural disasters, government action, or widespread internet outages, provided the affected Party gives prompt notice and uses reasonable efforts to resume performance.

**11.9 Counterparts; Electronic Signatures.** This Agreement may be executed in counterparts, each of which shall constitute an original. Electronic signatures (including via DocuSign or similar platforms) shall be deemed valid and binding.

**11.10 Headings.** Section headings are for convenience only and shall not affect the interpretation of this Agreement.

**11.11 Relationship of Parties.** Nothing in this Agreement creates a partnership, joint venture, employment relationship, or agency between the Parties. Each Party is an independent contractor responsible for its own actions, obligations, and liabilities.
